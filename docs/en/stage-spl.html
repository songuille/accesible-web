Stage SPL (iOS)

SPL meter for iPhone and iPad: dB(A)/dB(C)/dB(Z), Fast/Slow/Impulse, Max/Min/Leq and calibration.

Stage SPL measures sound pressure level in the room with the iPhone mic or an external mic. A/C/Z weighting, time constants, big readout, VU/peak/bar modules and calibration against a meter. For live guidance; not a certified IEC 61672 replacement.

Features

  • Live SPL readout with session Max, Min and Leq.
  • dB(A), dB(C) and dB(Z) weighting; Fast, Slow and Impulse times.
  • Cycling modules: analog VU, LED peak meter and level bar.
  • Offset calibration against a sound level meter or calibrator (94 dB @ 1 kHz).
  • iPhone microphone or external input (USB / Lightning / interface).
  • Configurable alert limit: blue below, red at or above.
  • Portrait or landscape; in landscape, big number + one module.
  • iPhone and iPad · iOS 16 or later.
